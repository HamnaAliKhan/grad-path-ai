Job-fit score (ranking aid only)

Fit = 55% skill match + 25% resume keyword match + 20% readiness points (Job Ready 100, Developing 60, Needs Improvement 25). Strong fit 75+, Good 55-74, Partial 35-54, Low below 35. It never makes hiring decisions.

Notes on the calculation

  • CGPA is divided by the student's grading scale (4.0, 5.0 or 10.0) and rounded to 4 decimal places before the tiers are applied.
  • Effective technical skills = confident skills + half of the skills being learned.
  • Values above the highest tier use the highest tier. Out-of-range input (for example a negative count or a CGPA above the scale) is rejected with a clear validation message.
  • Career skill requirements are illustrative examples, not verified employer requirements.
